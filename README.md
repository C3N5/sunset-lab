# Sunset Lab

Interactive sunset horizon and Earth-radius simulator.

Explore how changing your viewing height reveals more of the horizon and how sunset timing can estimate a planet's radius.

**Open the app:** Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. The app is self-contained, offline, and requires no installation.

## Science

For a spherical planet, the horizon dip angle at eye height `h` is `acos(R/(R+h))`. For two heights `h1` and `h2`, the dip difference is related to a measured sunset delay by an *assumed* apparent solar vertical angular speed. This simplification does not model latitude, solar declination, atmospheric refraction, terrain or weather. Results are educational approximations.
